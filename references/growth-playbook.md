# BoomX 2026 X Growth Playbook

The comprehensive growth strategy, cadence guidelines, and engagement loops used by top creators.

---

## 1. The Post-Post-Mortem Protocol

Never let a winning or losing tweet fade without extracting the growth rule.

When reviewing a post's 24-hour performance:
1. **Calculate Baseline Ratio**:
   - `Impressions / Baseline Median`
   - `Replies / Baseline Median`
   - `Bookmarks / Baseline Median`
2. **Identify the Primary Lever**:
   - **Hook Lever**: Did the first line deliver a concrete result with zero setup?
   - **Structure Lever**: Was it a numbered breakdown of mistakes or a contrarian truth?
   - **Conversation Lever**: Did an open prompt spark an ongoing thread in the comments?
3. **Formulate the Repeat Rule**:
   - *Example*: "Numbered lessons from our own billing mistakes scored 3.1x impressions. Action: Draft two more teardowns in this exact shape."

## 3. High-Value Reply & Engagement Routine

Top creators do not just broadcast—they farm discovery through comments on accounts with active target audiences.

1. **Feed Curation**:
   - Monitor high-traffic accounts in your niche (5k–100k followers).
   - Filter for posts published within the last 30 minutes with under 15 replies.
2. **Reply Tone Personas**:
   - **Insightful**: Adds a missing layer of tactical depth or data.
   - **Contrarian**: Respectfully questions an assumption with an alternative scenario.
   - **Analytical**: Quotes a specific metric or benchmark.
3. **The Anti-Self-Promotion Rule**:
   - Never pitch your product in a reply. The profile click and bio link handle conversion.

---

## 4. X CRM & Lead Signals

Convert timeline activity into customer conversations without spam:

1. **Sentiment Slicing**:
   - Scan replies to question posts or competitor announcements.
   - Target accounts asking about: *pricing, integrations, switching tools, bugs, hiring*.
2. **Card-Based DM Approval**:
   - Draft tailored 1-to-1 DMs referencing the recipient's exact post or bio.
   - Always present a review card before sending: *"Nothing sends until confirmed."*
3. **Soft Value Delivery**:
   - Share a relevant resource, sheet, or short video clip rather than an immediate sales pitch.
