# SuperX Viral Hook Formulas & Swipe Templates

Derived from SuperX's inspiration library and performance telemetry.

---

## 1. Numbered Lessons from Mistakes (Highest Retweet Rate)
*Pattern: Authentic failure + concrete timeframe or dollar amount.*

- "The [X] [topic] mistakes that cost me [time/money]:"
- "[X] things I got completely wrong about [industry/skill]:"
- "The [X] decisions I would undo if I started [company/project] over:"
- "I spent $[amount] trying to [goal]. Here is what actually worked:"
- "We made [error] for [duration]. One change solved it in [days]:"

---

## 2. Pattern Interrupt / Instant Cold Open (Highest Dwell Time)
*Pattern: Direct counter-intuitive thesis with zero preamble.*

- "[Topic] is not [conventional belief]. It is [contrarian reality]."
- "Whatever you do, do NOT [common action] until you know this:"
- "Most people think [X] is about [A]. In reality, it's 100% [B]."
- "Stop [habit]. Start [counter-intuitive action]."
- "If you cannot explain your [product/service] in 7 words, your product is broken."

---

## 3. Data-Backed Case Study (Highest Bookmark Rate)
*Pattern: Hard metric before/after + specific mechanism.*

- "We changed [single variable]. [Metric] jumped from [X]% to [Y]% in [timeframe]."
- "How [brand/creator] went from [Point A] to [Point B] without [common cost/tactic]:"
- "The exact teardown of our $[amount] [launch/campaign] (all numbers public):"
- "We analyzed [number] top [items]. Here are the [X] patterns that won:"

---

## 4. Conversation / Debate Provocations (Highest Heavy Ranker Reply Multiplier)
*Pattern: Polarizing trade-off that demands opinions from experienced practitioners.*

- "Hot take: [Unpopular opinion about industry norm]. Agree or disagree?"
- "Would you rather have [High Metric A with Downside B] or [Low Metric C with Upside D]?"
- "What is the single biggest red flag when [interviewing/evaluating/buying] [role/product]?"
- "What is an industry rule that everyone follows but you think is complete nonsense?"

---

## 5. Creator Custom Rules (Anti-Slop Directives)
*Rules enforced by SuperX's personal voice engine:*

1. **Never use emojis** in hooks unless representing bullet categories.
2. **Never ask rhetorical questions** as opening hooks ("Have you ever wondered...?").
3. **No corporate throat-clearing** ("In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "A thread 🧵").
4. **Concrete numbers beat adjectives** ("increased conversion by 14%" vs "massively improved conversion").
5. **Short punchy cadence**: 1–2 sentences per stanza maximum.
